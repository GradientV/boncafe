## Menu

- **Food, names, descriptions, prices:** the café's delivery menu (Grubhub/DoorDash, mirrored on res-discover.com "Updated 2026"). Prices on Grubhub and DoorDash matched the ones used here (e.g. Bulgogi Cheese Panini $15.95, Bulgogi Bibimbap $18, Chicken Club Panini $14.95). Both apps note prices "may be lower in-store", so **in-store prices may differ**.
- **Newer names** from the café's Instagram menu board (Oct 9, 2025) were used where they differ: "Bulgogi Bibimbap" (was "Bulgogi and Vegetable Bibim Rice"), "Royal Bulgogi Tteokbokki" (was "Gun-JJun Tteokbokki"), "Bon Café Premium Beef Burger" (was "Specialty Burger"). Spelling was cleaned up ("portabella" → portobello, "Avoado", "belluy", etc.) and the dated phrase "oriental chipotle mayo" became "chipotle mayo".
- **"Served with…" notes** per section are from DoorDash.
- **Japanese lunch & dinner:** DoorDash shows the section ("Available 12:00 pm – 8:00 pm, Monday – Saturday only") and features Vegetable Tempura $12. The Oyakodon & Vegetable Tempura set ($19.95) is from an Instagram special (Oct 27, 2025). The rest of that section, and the "Korean Entrées" section, didn't load and **are not on the site yet**.
- **Drinks** are from the Instagram drink board (Oct 9, 2025; the image is cropped, so a few drinks at the top and bottom may be missing), plus Caramel Latte, Taro Milk Tea and Hot Chocolate from the 2022 interview / listings, and Strawberry Matcha Latte from a customer review. **No drink prices were published** except smoothies ($7.95).
- **Soups:** soup of the day $6.95 with healthy bread (Instagram, Oct 27, 2025). Clam chowder bread bowl **$14**, "comes with bacon on top" (Yelp review by Fateha A., Feb 25, 2026). Shrimp & corn chowder in a bread bowl (Google review), price unknown.
- **Donkatsu Donburi Set** ("Crispy Pork & Vegetable Tempura Set, 12–8 PM / Mon–Sat only"): from a customer's Yelp photo caption (Feb 2026); price unknown.
- **"Beef Steak Taco Bowl"** is on the Instagram board but nowhere else, so it's left off until we know what's in it and the price.
- **"Meatless" tags** were applied only where no meat or seafood is listed. Kimchi items aren't tagged (kimchi often has fish sauce), and the allergy note says so.

## Confirm with the client

1. **Hours.** Google: Mon–Sat 7:30 AM – 7 PM, Sun 7:30 AM – 5 PM. Instagram bio: 8 AM – 7 PM. Grubhub pickup: 8 AM – 7 PM. Oct 2025 chalkboard: 7:30 AM – 9 PM daily. DoorDash's Japanese section runs until 8 PM. Which is right today?
2. **Phone.** (929) 708-2678 (Google) or (646) 963-3156 (Grubhub)? Or both (one for orders)?
3. **Owner details.** OK to name Shana on the site? Anything she'd like to add (where she's from, why "Bon", the team)? Is Francine still involved?
4. **Prices.** Are the delivery-app prices the same as in-store? Drink prices and sizes?
5. **Japanese lunch & dinner and Korean entrées.** The full list for both sections.
6. **Beef Steak Taco Bowl.** What's in it, and the price.
7. **Soups.** Chowder bread bowl price; is soup of the day still $6.95?
8. **Printing.** Still offered at $1 / 50¢ / students 25¢?
9. **Coffee basics.** Espresso, americano and cappuccino aren't on the posted board. Add them?
10. **Catering or large orders?** Not mentioned anywhere; worth a page if they do it.
11. **Photos.** The draft uses stock placeholders. Get 10–15 of their own (dishes, drinks, the room, the storefront, Shana), or book a quick shoot. Also confirm the DoorDash bulgogi photo is theirs to use.
12. **Domain.** `SITE_URL` in `src/data/site.ts` is set to `boncafeetrestaurant.com`, which was available on Oct 30, 2026 (a registry check quoted $16 for the first year). Not registered yet; availability can change.
13. **Logo.** The site uses a typographic BON CAFÉ / ET RESTAURANT wordmark and a "BC" monogram icon. Their current logo (a clip-art croissant with script lettering) is on Grubhub; ask which they want.
